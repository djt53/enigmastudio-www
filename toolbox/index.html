toolbox

A home for all your tools. Catalog what you own, and lend to neighbours nearby.

toolbox starts as a simple, private catalog of your tools and home items. Add things by photo, barcode, or a quick note, tag them, and find them again in seconds. It is useful on day one, with no network required.

When you are ready, mark an item as available and nearby neighbours can discover it, ask to borrow, and arrange a handoff, all inside the app.

What you can do

  • Catalog everything. Photos, barcode scanning, tags, notes, and fast search across your whole collection.
  • Lend to neighbours. Share a tool, handle borrow requests, and track what is out and when it is due back.
  • Share a household. Keep one catalog across a family, with attribution for who added what.
  • Keep your location private. Others see only an approximate distance, never your address. Your exact pickup spot is shared only when you agree to a specific lend.
