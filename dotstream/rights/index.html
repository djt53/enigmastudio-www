dotstream — Stations & Rights

Last updated: November 7, 2026

dotstream is a curated radio tuner for iPhone. It plays a small, hand-picked set of live internet-radio stations directly from each station’s own official public stream.

dotstream does not host, store, record, transcode, re-stream, or modify any audio, and it does not remove or replace station advertising or identification. Station names, logos, trademarks, and audio content are the property of the respective stations and rights holders. A listing in dotstream does not imply affiliation or endorsement.

Requests and removals

If you are a station or a rights holder and would like your stream added, changed, or removed, email david@enigmastudio.app. We honour removal requests promptly, typically within 48 hours.

Copyright notices

To report content you believe infringes your copyright, email david@enigmastudio.app with:

  1. your contact details;
  2. identification of the copyrighted work;
  3. the station or stream at issue in dotstream;
  4. a statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is unauthorized;
  5. a statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate and you are authorized to act for the rights holder;
  6. your physical or electronic signature.

We will remove or disable the identified stream on receipt of a valid notice.

Who operates dotstream
