dotstream Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

dotstream is a radio tuner for iPhone. This page explains what it does with your information. The short version: dotstream has no accounts and shows no ads. It uses a privacy-respecting analytics tool to understand how the app is used — anonymous usage data only, never tied to your real-world identity and never used to track you across other apps.

Who operates dotstream

dotstream is built and operated by Enigma Studio Inc., an Ontario, Canada software studio.

What we collect

  • No account. dotstream has no sign-in and no user accounts, so we never collect your name, email, or contacts.
  • Anonymous usage analytics. We use PostHog, a product-analytics tool, to understand how the app is used — for example which bands and features people open. This includes standard device and app information (such as device model, operating-system version, and app version) and an anonymous identifier generated on your device. It is not linked to your real-world identity, and we do not use it to track you across other apps or websites.
  • No advertising, no selling. We show no ads, use no advertising identifiers, and never sell your data.
  • On your device only. Your settings and saved presets (the MY band) are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.

Analytics processor

Our analytics are processed by PostHog on US-based servers, on our behalf. If you use dotstream from outside the United States, this anonymous usage information is transferred to and processed in the US, which may have different data-protection laws than your country of residence.

Streaming

When you play a station, your device connects directly to that station’s own stream server. We do not proxy, record, or log your listening. As with any internet streaming, the station or its stream host may receive your IP address and standard connection information, governed by that station’s own policies — not ours.

Children

dotstream is not directed at children. The usage analytics it collects are anonymous and are not used to identify anyone.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected in the “Last updated” date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or want your analytics data handled differently? Contact david@enigmastudio.app.
