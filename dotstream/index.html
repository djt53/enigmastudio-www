dotstream

A small radio. Pick a genre, get a handful of hand-picked live stations from around the world, and switch between them by hand.

Most radio apps hand you sixty thousand stations and a search bar. dotstream hands you a few good ones. Pick a band — Jazz, Ambient, Electronic, Indie, and more — and you get a small set of curated live stations. Tap a preset. When you’ve had enough, tap another. That is the whole app.

No personalization, no endless scroll, no feed deciding what you hear next. Just live radio, made by people, playing right now in a city that isn’t yours.

A radio tuner, not a broadcaster

dotstream plays each station’s own official public stream directly from its servers. We do not host, record, or alter any audio, and each station’s ads and IDs play untouched. Station names, logos, and content belong to the stations. To add, change, or remove a station, see the stations & rights page.
