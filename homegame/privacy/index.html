HomeGame Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

HomeGame is an app for hosting home poker nights — a tournament clock, chip calculator, ledger, side-pot solver, and odds trainer. This page explains what information HomeGame collects, why, and how it's handled, in plain language.

The short version: HomeGame has no accounts and no sign-in. Everything you create — your games, players, chip sets, and history — stays on your own device. We collect only anonymous usage analytics to understand how the app is used. We don't sell your data, and HomeGame does not involve real-money gambling of any kind.

Who operates HomeGame

HomeGame is built and operated by Enigma Studio Inc., a software studio based in Ontario, Canada.

Information we collect

Your game data stays on your device

The games you set up — chip sets, blind structures, buy-ins and re-buys, payouts, side-pot hands, and any player names you type in — are stored locally on your iPhone (in the app's own storage). This information is not transmitted to us or to anyone else. It stays on your device, and removing the app removes it.

Analytics and usage data

We use PostHog (a US-based analytics provider) to understand how the app is used. This includes product-usage events — such as which screens are viewed and actions like "new game started," "clock started," or "trainer session completed" — along with standard device and app metadata (for example OS version, app version, and device model), associated with a randomly generated identifier for your app install.

These analytics events do not include the content you enter — player names, buy-in amounts, payouts, or any other game data never leave your device. HomeGame does not collect your location, your contacts, your email, or any account information (there is no account).

Notifications

If you grant notification permission, HomeGame schedules notifications locally on your device to alert you when a blind level is about to change. These are generated on-device by iOS. HomeGame does not operate a push-notification server and does not send any notification token or device identifier to us for this purpose.

How we use this information

We use the anonymous analytics above only to understand which features are used and to improve the app. We do not use it to identify you, and we do not build advertising profiles.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. The only third party that receives any data is PostHog, our analytics processor, which processes the anonymous usage events described above on our behalf. We do not share data with advertisers or data brokers.

Children

HomeGame is a utility for running home poker tournaments and is not directed at children. It does not involve real-money gambling. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Notifications: turn them on or off any time in iOS Settings → HomeGame → Notifications.
  • Your data: because your game data lives only on your device, deleting the app permanently removes all of it.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
