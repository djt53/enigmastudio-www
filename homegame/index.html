HomeGame

Everything you need to host a home poker night, in one app.

HomeGame turns your phone into the dealer's box. Build a balanced chip stack for any number of players, run a full tournament blind clock that keeps going when your screen is locked, track buy-ins and pay out the winners, settle a messy all-in with the side-pot calculator, and sharpen your game with a built-in pot-odds trainer.

It all works on your own phone, with no account and no network required. Set up a game in a minute and get to the table.

What's inside

  • Tournament clock. Generate a blind schedule from your target length, then run a full-screen countdown with sound, haptics, and a Lock Screen Live Activity. Blinds go up on time, even when your phone is asleep.
  • Chip breakdown builder. Tell it your chip set and starting stack and it builds a clean, makeable per-player breakdown that colors up as the blinds grow.
  • Ledger & payouts. Track buy-ins and re-buys, set the prize split, and see exactly who gets paid what.
  • Side-pot calculator. Play out a tricky hand action by action and let HomeGame handle dead money, uncalled bets, and split pots.
  • Pot-odds trainer. Practice reading pot odds and equity, or use the quick calculator at the table.

Private by design

HomeGame has no accounts and no sign-in. Your games, players, and history live only on your device. HomeGame does not play for real money and does not involve any wagering inside the app.
