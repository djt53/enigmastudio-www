Common questions

The blinds-up alert didn't make a sound while my phone was locked.

HomeGame plays a sound and vibrates when a blind level changes, even on the Lock Screen. If you don't hear it, check that HomeGame is allowed to make sounds in iOS Settings → HomeGame → Notifications (Sounds on), that your ringer isn't silenced, and that Focus / Do Not Disturb isn't suppressing alerts. The on-screen countdown and in-app alert always fire regardless.

Does the clock keep running when I lock my phone or switch apps?

Yes. The blind schedule is anchored to real time and HomeGame schedules the level-change alerts in advance, so blinds go up on time whether the app is in the foreground, backgrounded, or your phone is locked. Open the app again and the clock resyncs to where it should be.

How do I change the blinds mid-game?

On the clock screen, tap a level to edit it, or open Edit Levels. Changes apply to the running clock immediately without restarting or losing your place.

Is this real-money gambling?

No. HomeGame is a utility for running an in-person poker tournament — a timer, a chip calculator, a ledger, and a practice trainer. There is no wagering or real-money play inside the app.

How do I delete my data?

HomeGame has no account, and all of your games and history are stored only on your device. Deleting the app permanently removes everything. See the privacy policy for detail.
